# Tapfolio Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: July 3, 2026**

Tapfolio ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website at `https://tapfolio.me` (the "Site") and the Tapfolio mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, how it is processed, and how you can exercise your rights, including account deletion.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when registering an account, customizing your networking profile card, or scanning cards.

*   **Account Registration Info:** Name, email address, password, and chosen custom username.
*   **Google Sign-In Profile Data:** Email address, full name, profile picture url, and Google SSO ID tokens.
*   **Card Profile Details:** Profile tagline, bio description, phone number, WhatsApp links, locations, list of professional links/socials, and avatars.
*   **NFC Networking Data:** Timestamps and logs of profiles you have tapped, connected with, or searched.

---

## 2. How Your Data is Used

*   To initialize and render your public professional networking profiles.
*   To log your networking connections and maintain a private connections history directory.
*   To power the public leaderboard ranks for premium card holders.
*   To authenticate your sessions securely.

---

## 3. Account and Data Deletion (GDPR / Play Store Compliance)

We believe in full transparency and data ownership. You have the right to delete your profile and account at any time.

### How to Delete Your Account In-App:
1.  Open the **Tapfolio** app.
2.  Tap the **More (3 dots)** icon in the upper-right corner of the AppBar.
3.  Tap **Delete Account**.
4.  Type the word **DELETE** in capital letters to confirm.
5.  Click the **Permanently Delete My Account** button.

### Alternative Deletion Request:
You may request manual deletion of all your databases records, files, and credentials by sending an email to **support@tapfolio.me**. We will process and wipe your data within 48 hours.

**Note:** Once deleted, your custom username will be released, your NFC cards will immediately stop linking, and your connection history cannot be retrieved.
